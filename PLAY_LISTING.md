## Full Description
Step into a tranquil Japanese wooden pavilion. ZenLITS brings the revered Nikoli pencil-and-paper logic puzzle **LITS** (ＬＩＴＳ / Nuruomino) to your device with handcrafted elegance, peaceful aesthetics, and procedural timber joinery acoustics (*Kigumi* 木組み).

### The Art of Kigumi Architecture
Partition temple rooms by placing exactly one 4-block tetromino into each outlined chamber:
1. **One Tetromino Per Chamber**: Every room must contain exactly 4 shaded timber blocks forming a valid tetromino.
2. **Valid Shapes (L, I, T, S)**: The 4 blocks must form an **L**, **I**, **T**, or **S** shape (rotations and reflections allowed). Solid 2×2 "O" blocks are strictly forbidden.
3. **No Touching Identical Shapes**: If two tetrominoes in adjacent rooms touch orthogonally (sharing an edge), they **cannot be of the same type** (e.g. an L cannot touch another L; diagonal contact is permitted).
4. **No 2×2 Solid Squares**: Shaded blocks cannot form a 2×2 square anywhere across the temple floor plan.
5. **Single Connected Network**: All shaded blocks across the entire temple must connect into a single continuous orthogonal structure.

### Key Features
- **50 Handcrafted Levels**: 5 difficulty tiers ranging from beginner Engawa (6x6) to master Daitokuji (10x10).
- **100% Unique Solutions**: Every puzzle is verified by deductive logic constraints. No guesswork required.
- **Procedural Acoustic Engine**: Authentic hinoki wood planings, sumi ink chisel taps, soft bamboo ticks, harmonic koto chords, and resonant Bonshō temple bells generated live through pure Web Audio synthesis.
- **Live Tetromino Classifier**: Real-time shape recognition automatically classifies and displays kanji glyphs (L, I, T, S) with soothing visual confirmation.
- **Intuitive Touch Controls**: Seamlessly tap or drag to place shaded timber blocks or mark open tatami floor dots.
- **Smart Deductive Hint Engine**: Receive gentle, logical guidance whenever you need insight into forced placements, 2×2 avoidance, or chamber options.
- **Unlimited Undo & Reset**: Solve at your own serene pace without timer anxiety.
- **Completely Offline & Private**: Zero advertisements, zero user tracking, zero data collection. Plays flawlessly without an internet connection.
